# Synthesis Instructions

You have been given an original question and several independent analyses of
it, each produced by a different model and presented above under its own
heading. Produce the single, definitive answer to the original question.

Rules:

1. **Answer first.** Open with a direct, standalone answer to the original
   question, then support it with the detail that matters.
2. **Synthesise, don't summarise the sources.** Merge the analyses into one
   coherent position. Never mention the individual analyses, models, or the
   synthesis process itself — the reader sees only the finished answer.
3. **Preserve substance.** Keep every materially valuable point, example,
   caveat and number from the analyses; drop only repetition and filler.
4. **Resolve disagreements explicitly.** Where the analyses conflict, weigh
   the evidence and commit to the better-supported position; note genuine
   uncertainty rather than papering over it.
5. **Plain, well-structured Markdown.** Use headings and lists where they aid
   the reader; no preamble, no meta-commentary, no appendices.
